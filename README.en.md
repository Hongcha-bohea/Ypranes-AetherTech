# Ypranes: Aether Tech

English | [中文](README.md)

A content mod for Mindustry, taking you to the next planet beyond the vanilla star system — **Ypranes**.

---

## Key Features

- **Brand New Energy System**: Original Aether-series products with unique transportation and synthesis methods.
- **Aether Production Chain**: Aether Extractor → Aether Crafter → Aether Compressor → Aether Generator. Four stages of processing, each building on the last.
- **Special Radioactive Resources**: Radioactive resources requiring special extraction. Using the wrong machinery will cause serious consequences.
- **Brand New Dynamic Events**: Risk and reward coexist — they can bring destruction, or a bountiful harvest. Perhaps... you can control these events and turn them into even greater profits?
- **Dual Factions**: Polaris and Shadow, a struggle over the fate of Ypranes.

---

## Progress

**Implemented:**

- Mod framework is up and running. Welcome dialog displays correctly.
- Resources: Aether Shard, Aether Energy (liquid), Aether Energy Cell, Aether Alloy, Tungsten.
- Buildings: Aether Extractor, Aether Crafter, Aether Compressor, Aether Generator, Radioactive Extractor, Aether Alloy Smelter.
- Full production chain: Shard → Energy → Cell → Power → Alloy.
- Chinese and English localization.

**Planned:**

- Aether turrets and defensive structures.
- Aether unit factories and new units.
- Ypranes planet generator, including Aether ore veins and radioactive veins.
- Dynamic event system, including meteor strikes and radiation leaks.
- Faction selection system, allowing players to join Polaris or Shadow.
- Nuclear tech line: extraction and utilization of Uranium, Plutonium, Radon, Tritium, and Argon.
